# Accessibility

Monocle Network is committed to making its services usable by as many people as possible. This page describes our accessibility goals, known barriers, and how to report accessibility problems.

## Goals

We aim to keep the touch points we control - the [website](https://monoclenetwork.org) and the [wiki](https://monocle.wiki) - accessible: navigable by keyboard, readable with adequate contrast and text sizing, and usable with assistive technology where feasible.

For the Monocle desktop application, accessibility improvements come upstream from Haveno. The application is intended for desktop use only, at this time.

## Known barriers

We want to be honest about current limitations:

- The Monocle application is desktop only; no mobile or screen-reader-optimized interface is available
- Accessibility of the application depends on upstream Haveno; we do not control it directly
- The website, wiki, support, and documentation are provided in English only - no other languages are available

Accessibility work is prioritized alongside other project work within the resources available. We cannot promise immediate remediation, but reports help us understand what matters most.

## Reporting an accessibility problem

Accessibility problems are not security issues - please report them as regular issues:

- On the [repository](https://github.com/orgs/monocle-network/repositories) concerned (for example, the website or wiki repository), if you know where the problem belongs
- In the [meta repository](https://github.com/monocle-network/meta) if you are unsure where it belongs

When reporting, describe what you were trying to do, what barrier you encountered, and any assistive technology you use. This helps us understand and reproduce the problem.
